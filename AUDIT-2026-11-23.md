**Key result.** The core safety model holds. Min-out comes from the vault's own balance changes against Chainlink. Each swap may pull at most `amountIn`. Approvals, including Permit2, are cleared after every swap, ETH can't leave the vault, and every swap path has `nonReentrant`. Together these mean even a malicious agent using an arbitrary-call router (Universal Router, 1inch, 0x) loses at most `maxSlippageBps × dailyCap` per day. Fuzz tests confirm this. The one Medium is the missing L2 sequencer uptime check on Base, which is now fixed.

## 4. Findings

### M-01: No Chainlink L2 sequencer uptime check on Base (Fixed)
- **Location:** `_price` (used by `executeSwap`, `quoteUsd` and `previewMinOut`).
- **Impact:** During or just after a Base sequencer outage, a stale price within `maxStale` is accepted. The suggested `ETH_USD_STALE` is 75 min. The agent can then swap against an out-of-date min-out and take value beyond the intended slippage.
- **Fix:** `sequencerFeed` (immutable) is set to `0xBCF85224fc0756B9Fa45aA7892530B47e10b6433` when `block.chainid == 8453`, and to address(0) elsewhere, with no signature changes. Prices revert with `SequencerDown` or `SequencerGracePeriod` (1h after a restart). I checked the feed live on Base.
- **Regression tests:** `test_M01_noSequencerFeedOffBase`, `test_M01_swapRevertsWhileSequencerDown`, `test_M01_swapRevertsInGracePeriodThenWorks`. Base fork swaps pass through the real feed.

### L-01: Agent can extract up to maxSlippageBps of the daily cap per day (Acknowledged, by design)
A malicious agent can route through its own pool or order at exactly min-out. `testFuzz_L01_dailyLossBoundedBySlippageTimesCap` confirms the loss is at most `slippage × dailyCap` (worst case 20%). Grant small slippage (≤1%) in practice.

### L-02: Suggested staleness is loose for Base (Acknowledged)
`ETH_USD_STALE` of 1h15m fits Ethereum's 1h heartbeat. Base ETH/USD has a 20 min heartbeat, so owners on Base can pass a tighter `maxStale` (for example 30 min) to `setFeed`.

### L-03: Feed decimals cached at setFeed (Acknowledged)
If an aggregator proxy is repointed to a feed with different decimals, prices are misread until the owner calls `setFeed` again. Unlikely for Chainlink USD feeds.

### L-04: Fee-on-transfer or rebasing tokens (Acknowledged)
Caps and min-out assume standard ERC-20s. Only allowlist plain tokens (USDC, WETH).

### I-01: Slither results triaged as benign (see Method).
### I-02: Refuted vectors (PoC tests)
- A router pulling another token, or more than `amountIn`, reverts, and allowances are 0 afterwards: `test_refute_routerCannotPullOtherTokens`, plus the existing overpull test.
- A router re-entering `executeSwap` or `wrapETH` hits `ReentrancyGuardReentrantCall`: `test_refute_reentrancyBlocked`.
- Output sent elsewhere is rejected by the balance-delta check (existing test). Permit2 allowance expires in 2 min and is reset to 0. Revoke bumps the epoch and wipes allowlists. Daily-window rollover math is correct.
### I-03: Router allowlist is the trust boundary
Arbitrary-call routers are safe only because of the balance-delta checks. Never allowlist a token address or a contract that holds vault approvals.
